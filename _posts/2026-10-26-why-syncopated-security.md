Syncopation is what happens when you put the accent where nobody expects it: on the "and" instead of the beat, on the off-beat instead of the one. It is the reason a groove feels alive instead of marching in place.

SYN is the first packet of a TCP handshake. The sequence is **SYN**, **SYN-ACK**, **ACK**: one side says hello, the other says hello back, and the first confirms. Three hits, and a connection exists.

## The off-beat is where the interesting things live

Most security work is about noticing what does not land where the pattern says it should. A login at an odd hour. A burst of traffic on a quiet port. A log that goes silent when it should be talking. In drumming terms, that is a hit on the "and," and a good listener hears it before everyone else does.

## And then there is the volume

I play drums, I read charts, and I like my music loud. Zero trust is the security model: never assume, always verify. Full volume is the rest of me. The name and the tagline are that pairing, written down.

The writing here will cover security work, movies, and heavy music, usually one at a time and occasionally all at once.
